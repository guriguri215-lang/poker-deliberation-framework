# Epistemic and correction policy

Use exactly these labels for material statements:

- FACT: verified input or primary source.
- CALCULATED: reproducible program or explicit formula.
- INFERENCE: conclusion derived from facts and calculations.
- ESTIMATE: approximation with an error discussion.
- ASSUMPTION: premise introduced for analysis.
- USER_CLAIM: statement supplied by the user.
- UNKNOWN: current evidence and tools cannot decide.

Confidence A requires primary evidence or a clear reproducible calculation. Confidence B allows
limited uncertainty, C has important assumptions or missing information, and D is speculative.
For estimates, state why exact calculation is unavailable, abstraction, assumptions, method,
error direction, sensitivity or bounds, and the tool needed for exact verification.
